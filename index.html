Manual values replace calculated totals and affect estimates. Clear an edited field or use Reset to return to calculation. Chances and penetration use fractions (0.25 = 25%); multipliers use 1 = 100%.

Recovery & defence assumptions

Regeneration is effective health/mana per second, including gain modifiers. Current mana % in Configuration controls missing-mana bonuses. Health regeneration resumes after the game’s regeneration delay; recovery is not counted in maximum-hit estimates. Resistances are ratings, not percentages; negative ratings amplify damage. Movement is a flat-terrain stat multiplier, not simulated travel speed. Maximum hits cover the initial landed hit only, excluding affliction ticks, avoidance, shields and damage-taken modifiers. Fire damage over time can kill after surviving the initial hit.

Attribute allocation inputs
